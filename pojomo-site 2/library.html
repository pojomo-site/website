Read at your level

Every story in the library is written for a specific level. Start where reading feels comfortable, not where it feels like work. When a level gets easy, move up.

Level 1First words
Level 2Everyday
Level 3Growing
Level 4Confident
Level 5Fluent
Level 6Advanced

Why stories?

A good story keeps you reading. The more you read and listen, the more English you acquire, so the library is built around stories people want to finish, not exercises they want to get through.
